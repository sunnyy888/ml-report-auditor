No-show model: summary. Data: all 20,000 labeled bookings, random 80/20 split by booking (16,000 train, 4,000 test). Model: gradient-boosted trees. Features: fare class, days booked ahead, party size, route, checked_in_online, bag fee paid, passenger's past no-show count. Test results (4,000 bookings): TP 184, FP 60, FN 216, TN 3,540. Accuracy 93.1%. Precision 0.82. Frequent flyers (5 or more bookings) make up 38% of all bookings. Summary: the model is 93% accurate and ready to ship. Feature importance shows that bag fees cause no-shows; dropping the bag fee would cut no-shows by a third.
